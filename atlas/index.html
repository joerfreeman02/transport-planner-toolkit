About ATLAS

ATLAS is development software within the EAS FORGE research and development programme.

In development

ATLAS — Automated Transport & Location Assessment System

ATLAS helps transport planners collect, check and present transport information for development sites using trusted data sources.

It retrieves transport information from trusted sources and performs repeatable calculations, so planners can spend less time on routine research and more time on professional assessment.
