About ATLAS

ATLAS — Automated Transport & Location Assessment System

ATLAS is EAS's internal transport-planning platform for faster, more consistent site assessment and research.

In development

Authoritative research, controlled workflow

ATLAS brings authoritative transport sources and controlled, repeatable calculations into one practical assessment workflow. It reduces repetitive research and makes the evidence behind each result easier to review.

ATLAS supports professional work; the transport planner checks the evidence, resolves uncertainty and retains responsibility for judgement.

EAS FORGE

Built through EAS's internal R&D programme

EAS FORGE develops focused consultancy software that makes established workflows faster and more consistent while keeping professional judgement with EAS staff.
