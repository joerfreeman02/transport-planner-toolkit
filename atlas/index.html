About ATLAS

ATLAS — Automated Transport & Location Assessment System

ATLAS is EAS's internal transport-planning platform for faster, more consistent site assessment and research.

In development

Authoritative research, controlled workflow

ATLAS brings authoritative transport sources and controlled, repeatable calculations into one practical assessment workflow. It reduces repetitive research and makes the evidence behind each result easier to review.

ATLAS supports professional work; the transport planner checks the evidence, resolves uncertainty and retains responsibility for judgement.

EAS FORGE

Built through EAS's internal R&D programme

Framework for Operational Research, Governance & Engineering

EAS FORGE develops focused consultancy software that makes established workflows faster and more consistent while keeping professional judgement with EAS staff.

Data acknowledgements

Trusted sources used today

Address search, maps and access routing use OpenStreetMap data. London stop discovery is provided by Transport for London. National stops use Department for Transport NaPTAN; timetables use the Department for Transport Bus Open Data Service and supplementary Traveline National Dataset (TNDS) information.

Build the Bus assessment

Choose a full nearby-stop assessment or ask ATLAS for the nearest logical stop group. London starts at 400 m and other areas at 700 m; you can change the radius before searching.

Nearest mode is based on routed walking access. ATLAS then keeps the complete logical stop group, including opposite directions or interchange stands where identified.
