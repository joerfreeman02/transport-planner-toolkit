Bus Assessment · Beta

Evidence-led transport assessment

The Bus module identifies nearby stops and presents available route, timetable and access evidence in a consistent assessment workflow.

It supports professional work; planners remain responsible for checking evidence, resolving uncertainty and exercising judgement before formal use.

Built through EAS's internal R&D programme

Framework for Operational Research, Governance & Engineering

Trusted sources used today

National stops use Department for Transport NaPTAN; timetables use the Department for Transport Bus Open Data Service and supplementary Traveline National Dataset (TNDS) information.
