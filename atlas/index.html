Authoritative research, controlled workflow

ATLAS brings authoritative transport sources and controlled, repeatable calculations into one practical assessment workflow. It reduces repetitive research and makes the evidence behind each result easier to review.

ATLAS supports professional work; the transport planner checks the evidence, resolves uncertainty and retains responsibility for judgement.

Data acknowledgements

Trusted sources used today

Address search, maps and access routing use OpenStreetMap data. London stop discovery is provided by Transport for London. National stops use Department for Transport NaPTAN; timetables use the Department for Transport Bus Open Data Service and supplementary Traveline National Dataset (TNDS) information.

How ATLAS works

From site to evidence in four steps

A consistent workflow for reviewable transport planning.

  1. 1Find your siteSearch or place the assessment point.
  2. 2AnalyseAssess nearby stops, routes and timetable evidence.
  3. 3Review resultsCheck maps, service summaries and Sources & Checks.
  4. 4Export & usePrepare a Word assessment and map for planning work.

Why planners use ATLAS

Trusted evidence. Clearer assessments.

Designed around real transport-planning review rather than automated decisions.

  • Authoritative evidenceSources and checked data remain visible.
  • Consistent outputsRepeatable methods support comparable site assessments.
  • Planner-led judgementReview flags and qualifications stay explicit.
  • Growing modular platformBus is the current ATLAS Beta assessment module.
